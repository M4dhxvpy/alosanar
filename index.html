Alosanar converts underutilized commercial real estate into data center infrastructure, unlocking new inventory for compute tenants.

Find out more about us

Alosanar creates value for both commercial real estate owners and compute tenants through a specialized operating model designed for today’s unprecedented demand for AI infrastructure. By governing a transparent ecosystem of best-qualified specialized organizations, Alosanar provides the technical validation, commercialization, design, construction, operations, financing, and tenant origination required to transform existing commercial real estate into market-ready data center opportunities.

For commercial real estate owners, this approach provides a practical pathway to participate in the rapidly growing data center market without requiring prior data center experience or building internal data center capabilities.

For compute tenants—including neo cloud providers, hyperscalers, cloud service providers, enterprises, and other AI infrastructure users—Alosanar unlocks access to new sites and power capacity beyond the limited inventory available through traditional, capacity-constrained data center markets.
