Alosanar converts underutilized commercial real estate into data center infrastructure, unlocking new inventory for compute tenants.

Find out more about us

Alosanar creates value for both the supply and demand sides of the data center market through an approach designed for today’s unprecedented need for compute infrastructure.

We identify and develop underutilized space, land, and power within existing commercial real estate assets by bringing the entire data center value chain to property owners, including site evaluation, design, construction, operations, financing, and tenant acquisition. This enables traditional commercial real estate owners to participate in the rapidly growing data center market without requiring prior data center experience.

For compute tenants—including neocloud providers, hyperscalers, cloud service providers, enterprises, and other infrastructure users—this approach unlocks access to new sites and power capacity beyond the limited inventory available from traditional, capacity-constrained data center providers.
