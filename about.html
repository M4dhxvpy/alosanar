About

Alosanar was founded in 2024 by Emmanuel Daniel, former Microsoft data center and global facilities executive. Emmanuel notably led expansion of the data center capacity from 200 to 500 facilities in 30 countries, while investing up to $2B per month and growing the data center team. Prior to that he spearheaded the smart buildings program for Microsoft globally including extensive physical, cultural and programmatic redevelopment of the East Campus headquarters in Redmond, WA.

A year later Emmanuel was joined by Tom Shircliff, who has 20 years experience in technology management consulting to large real estate investors, owners and operators including REIT, insurance companies, pension funds, and F500. Tom has a deep knowledge of the commercial real estate environment and technology scaling including site and building conversion.

This combined experience includes an extensive global ecosystem of partners in every discipline of the development and operations lifecycle that has been reassembled for a delivery model that yields speed and scale even in an environment of scarcity.

The company has assembled the most experienced executives and solution partners in data center, artificial intelligence, facility, technology, scaling, and operations. Alosanar is operating in a variety of capacities, including consulting, design, construction management, BESS solutions, financing and joint venture partnerships.
