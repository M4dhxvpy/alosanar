Energy

The well-publicized energy concerns surrounding data center demand often overlook a significant mitigating factor. While some greenfield developments and projects with highly specific location requirements face legitimate power constraints, there are, in reality, gigawatts of available capacity distributed across existing commercial buildings and properties throughout the United States and around the world. This capacity includes existing utility interconnections, untapped grid availability, and opportunities to augment available power through on-site generation and hybrid energy solutions.

Alosanar has assembled a large and growing portfolio of properties through site rights, referrals, lease opportunities, and acquisitions that provide access to this diverse and often overlooked capacity.
